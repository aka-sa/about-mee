FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I apply for a research internship?

Visit our Opportunities page to review open positions, then submit an application through this contact form selecting "Internship Application" as your inquiry type.

Do you offer remote research positions?

Yes. Most of our research associate and volunteer positions support fully remote collaboration. Internships offer both remote and hybrid options.

How can universities partner with Scholaro?

We welcome joint research programs, co-supervision agreements, and shared infrastructure partnerships. Contact us at research@scholaro.org with your proposal.

Are your research resources free to use?

All templates, guides, and toolkits on our Resources page are freely available under Creative Commons licensing for academic use.
