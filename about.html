About Scholaro Research Labs

A global research, innovation, publication, and technology development ecosystem dedicated to advancing knowledge and creating real-world impact.

Our Mission

Advancing Knowledge Through Interdisciplinary Research

Scholaro Research Labs exists at the intersection of rigorous academic inquiry and practical innovation. We believe that the most transformative breakthroughs emerge when researchers from diverse disciplines collaborate with a shared commitment to impact.

From artificial intelligence and accessibility to healthcare intelligence and cybersecurity, our six research laboratories pursue questions that matter — and deliver solutions that scale.

History

Our Journey

2019

Foundation

Scholaro Research Labs founded with a vision to democratize research tools and accelerate interdisciplinary discovery.

2021

Lab Expansion

Launch of AI Research Lab and Accessibility Innovation Lab, establishing our dual focus on intelligence and inclusion.

2023

Global Partnerships

Strategic alliances with 15 universities and 10 industry partners across North America, Europe, and Asia-Pacific.

2024

Flagship Projects

Research Ecosystem

Six specialized laboratories, 120+ publications, and a growing community of 200+ researchers worldwide.
