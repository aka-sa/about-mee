Active Healthcare AI

MediLens

Domain: Medical Imaging & Clinical Diagnostics

Objective: AI-powered medical imaging analysis platform for early disease detection across radiology, pathology, and ophthalmology, enabling clinicians to identify conditions at stages when intervention is most effective.

PyTorch Computer Vision DICOM FHIR Federated Learning

Status: Deployed in 12 hospital networks across 4 countries.

Future Scope: Multi-modal diagnostic platform with federated learning for privacy-preserving hospital networks, expanding to genomics integration and real-time surgical guidance.

Active Accessibility

Accessibility AI

Domain: Assistive Technology & Inclusive Design

Objective: Real-time AI assistance for visual, auditory, and cognitive accessibility across digital platforms, ensuring universal access to information and services.

NLP Speech Synthesis Computer Vision WCAG 2.2

Status: Integrated with 50+ web platforms, serving 2M+ users monthly.

Future Scope: Universal accessibility layer integrable with any web or mobile application via SDK, with offline capabilities for low-connectivity regions.
