Research Laboratories

Six specialized laboratories where interdisciplinary teams pursue breakthrough discoveries and translate research into deployable solutions.

AI Research Lab

Pioneering the next generation of intelligent systems through foundation models, multimodal learning, and responsible AI.

Our AI Research Lab develops cutting-edge machine learning architectures and deploys them in real-world contexts. Research areas include large language models, computer vision, reinforcement learning, and AI alignment.

Foundation Models

Training and fine-tuning domain-specific LLMs for scientific and medical applications.

Multimodal AI

Integrating text, vision, and audio for comprehensive understanding systems.

AI Safety

Robustness, interpretability, and alignment research for trustworthy deployment.

♿

Accessibility Innovation Lab

Designing AI-powered assistive technologies that empower people with disabilities and promote universal digital access.

We believe technology should be accessible to everyone. Our lab develops real-time captioning, screen reader enhancement, cognitive accessibility tools, and automated WCAG compliance systems.

Assistive AI

Real-time visual and auditory assistance powered by deep learning.

Inclusive Design

Human-centered design frameworks for universally accessible products.

Compliance Automation

AI-driven WCAG 2.2 auditing and remediation for web platforms.

🏥

Healthcare Intelligence Lab

Advancing clinical decision support, medical imaging AI, and population health analytics for better patient outcomes.

Our healthcare researchers collaborate with clinicians and hospitals worldwide to develop AI systems that augment medical expertise while maintaining the highest standards of privacy and ethics.

Medical Imaging

Deep learning for radiology, pathology, and ophthalmology diagnostics.

Clinical NLP

Extracting insights from electronic health records and clinical notes.

Predictive Analytics

Risk stratification and early intervention models for population health.

🔐

Cybersecurity Research Lab

Developing next-generation threat detection, privacy-preserving systems, and secure AI infrastructure.

As cyber threats evolve, our lab applies machine learning and formal methods to build adaptive defense systems. Research spans threat intelligence, zero-trust architectures, and adversarial ML.

Threat Intelligence

Graph-based analysis of attack patterns and automated IOC extraction.

Privacy Engineering

Differential privacy, homomorphic encryption, and secure multi-party computation.

Adversarial ML

Defending AI systems against poisoning, evasion, and extraction attacks.

📊

Data Science Lab

Building scalable data pipelines, open research datasets, and reproducible scientific computing infrastructure.

Data is the foundation of modern research. Our lab creates curated datasets, develops analytics frameworks, and establishes best practices for reproducible, open science.

Open Datasets

Curated, annotated datasets for AI, healthcare, and accessibility research.

Knowledge Graphs

Semantic networks connecting research entities, citations, and concepts.

Reproducibility

Tools and frameworks ensuring research results are verifiable and replicable.

🚀

Emerging Technologies Lab

Exploring quantum algorithms, neuromorphic computing, edge AI, and next-generation human-computer interfaces.

Our forward-looking lab investigates technologies that will define the next decade of computing — from quantum machine learning to brain-computer interfaces and decentralized AI.

Quantum ML

Hybrid quantum-classical algorithms for optimization and simulation.

Edge AI

Efficient on-device inference for IoT, wearables, and mobile systems.

Neuromorphic Computing

Brain-inspired architectures for ultra-low-power intelligent systems.
